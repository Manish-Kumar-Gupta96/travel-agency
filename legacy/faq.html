General Travel Questions Answered By Our Concierges

How do I plan and book a custom itinerary?

You can start by submitting an inquiry via our Booking Page or contacting a travel advisor directly. We will schedule a brief phone call or video consultation to discuss your destination choices, dates, guest counts, and special needs (such as helicopter transfers or private chefs). Following this, we compile a detailed day-by-day itinerary proposal for your review.

Are flights included in the starting package prices?

Our starting package prices generally exclude international flight tickets to give our guests flexibility in choosing their preferred airlines, routes, and cabin classes. However, we can assist in booking first-class commercial tickets or chartering private jets as an add-on service.

What is your cancelation policy for boutique resorts?

Because we book luxury boutique resorts and private yachts, cancelation terms vary by location and season. Generally, bookings canceled more than 60 days before travel qualify for a full refund (minus credit processing and admin fees). Cancelations within 30 days are subject to hotel hold policies. We highly recommend obtaining comprehensive luxury travel insurance to cover unexpected schedule changes.

Can you organize trips for corporate groups?

Yes, our dedicated events team plans executive corporate retreats, team building workshops, and private group tours. We handle block hotel bookings, private dining room reservations, conference hall facilities, charter flights, and group local transport arrangements.

Do you offer direct assistance during the trip?

Absolutely. When you book a package, you are assigned a dedicated concierge who is available 24/7 via WhatsApp or phone. They can handle last-minute dinner reservations, change transfer timings, arrange guides, and assist with any medical or travel needs.
