Booking Policies

Here is what you need to know about reserving vacations with TravelNest concierge.

Bespoke Draft Proposal

Upon form submission, a travel advisor compiles your custom proposal package within 12 hours. There are no fees to compile or modify drafts.

Secure Deposits

Confirming your travel plan requires a 30% deposit. Final balances must be paid 30 days prior to check-in dates.

Flexible Rebooking

Changes to travel dates are allowed free of charge up to 45 days before check-in, depending on hotel seasonal availability.
